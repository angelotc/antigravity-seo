# Maps & Geo-Grid Visibility

## 1. Geo-grid rank scan (manual methodology)
The map pack ranks differently per coordinates. Sample a grid around the service area:
- Center + 8 points (N/NE/E/SE/S/SW/W/NW) at ~1–2km and ~5km radii for metro areas.
- At each point, query `{{service}} near me` / `{{service}} {{city}}` and record rank 1–20+ or "not listed".
- Present as a color-coded grid artifact; clusters of red far from the business location indicate a proximity problem (fix: more local signals, not more pages).

## 2. GBP audit checklist
- Categories (primary + secondary), services list complete with descriptions
- Photos: logo, cover, team, work samples; geotagged where appropriate
- Posts: ≥1 per week; offer/updates with UTM links
- Q&A: owner-answered, seeded with real questions
- Booking/appointment links working; messaging enabled with <24h response
- Address/hours accurate including holiday hours

## 3. Review intelligence
- Volume vs top-3 competitors, average rating, response rate, recency velocity
- Mine review text (competitors' too) for repeated phrases → landing page copy and FAQ material
- Flag policy-risky practices (review gating, incentivizing) — recommend removal paths
